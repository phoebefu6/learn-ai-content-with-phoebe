# Official course map - learn-ai-content-with-phoebe

**Course:** AI + Content Generation - generate content at scale, without the slop
**Scope:** Enterprise content operations - the craft AND the ops of making content with AI at scale: ideas + briefs, one-idea-to-many-formats repurposing, long-form, short-form/social, visual, video/audio, the pipeline/workflow, quality/anti-slop editing, and governance/IP. Distinct from AI + Marketing (content INSIDE the funnel) and AI + Branding (the brand system). This course = the content engine.
**Arc:** Running-project - the creator track builds **Cadence's content engine** end to end (series continuity: branded in AI+Branding, campaigned in AI+Marketing, now the ongoing content ops). Two-track: leader track (a1-a6, heads of content / brand + comms) + creator track (b1-b10, hands-on production).
**Coverage bar:** ~80% of mapped sources' working content per session; certificates/paid tooling stay official.
**Build mode:** course-taking loop PAUSED (built direct from verified sources, no learner-notes step).

## Market context (verify before delivery - moves fast)

- The defining 2026 shift: multimodal production at scale - text, image, video, audio from a SINGLE brief, then refined with brand controls + performance feedback.
- AI compresses multi-channel content production timelines by ~60-70%.
- The bottleneck moves from producing content to keeping it distinctive + on-brand (anti-slop) and governing IP/disclosure at volume.

## Hard rails (bake into every relevant page)

- **"Own your voice and the claims."** No fabricated facts, stats, or quotes; the human edit is non-negotiable; distinctiveness beats volume - AI content that reads as AI (slop) fails.
- **The anti-slop standard:** AI drafts fast, but a human editor makes it specific, true, and unmistakably yours before it publishes. Volume without the edit is how a brand becomes noise.
- **IP + disclosure + provenance rail:** copyright and likeness risk in AI text/image/video/voice; disclose AI content where required (FTC); use rights-clear inputs; adopt provenance/authenticity practices.
